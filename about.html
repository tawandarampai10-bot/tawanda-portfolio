GET TO KNOW ME

My Story.

Every journey has a beginning. Mine started in a small town in the Free State, with curiosity, big dreams and an interest in understanding how things work.

Where It All Began

I was born and raised in Rosendal, a small town in the Free State, South Africa. Growing up, football was a big part of my childhood. I was the kind of boy who always had a football with him.

But there was another interest growing alongside my love for football: technology. I was curious about computers and the possibilities they offered.

Discovering Programming

My interest in technology became more serious when I discovered programming and realised that code could be used to create websites, applications and useful digital solutions.

In 2024, while I was still in matric, I started learning C++. I used online resources such as W3Schools and YouTube to explore programming and prepare myself for the technology journey ahead.

That experience helped me realise that I didn't just want to use technology. I wanted to understand how it works and learn how to build it myself.

Where I'm Going

My ambition is to become a skilled software developer and eventually a technology entrepreneur.

I want to keep improving my technical abilities, work on meaningful projects, solve real problems and build technology that can make a difference.

I'm still learning, experimenting and growing. This portfolio is part of that journey.
